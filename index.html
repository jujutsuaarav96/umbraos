How it works

Simple idea.
Powerful result.

UmbraOS sits on your computer like a second brain. It reads your screen, understands your apps, and works for you — locally or in the cloud, your choice.

01

Runs where you want

Run AI locally on your machine for maximum privacy, or use the cloud for more power. You choose what goes where.

02

Use any AI model

Connect your own API keys for Claude, GPT-4o, or others. Or use built-in local models — no API key required.

03

Learns as you work

Remembers your preferences, files, and habits. The more you use it, the smarter it gets — without asking twice.

Features

Everything you need.

Private AI that actually does things — not just chats. Here's what UmbraOS can do.

01

Run multiple AI agents at once

One agent watches your email. Another writes code. Another organises files. They all work at the same time without slowing each other down.

Isolated processes · Shared memory · Under 150 MB RAM

02

Bring your own AI models

Use Claude, GPT-4o, or any provider with your own API keys. No middleman, no markup. Or use free built-in models.

BYOK · Hybrid cloud + local · Zero markup

03

Pay less for AI

Smart compression cuts your AI costs. Removes filler, compresses context, and only sends what matters to the model.

Smart pruning · Prompt compression · Near-zero cost

04

Turn tasks into apps

Teach it once, it remembers. Repetitive tasks get compiled into fast native programs — no more paying for AI every time.

Auto code generation · Local .exe/.dll · Instant speed

05

Encrypted password vault

Store passwords, API keys, and tokens on your machine. Encrypted with your hardware — only you can access them.

Hardware encryption · AES-256 · Your device only

06

Hidden background screen

A secret second desktop where AI works for you — opening files, browsing, filling spreadsheets — without taking over your screen.

Virtual desktop · Zero interruption · Always hidden

07

Control from your phone

Check on your AI agents from your phone. See what they're doing, give new tasks, all through a secure connection.

Phone app · Direct connection · Secure

08

Memory that never forgets

Your preferences, files, and habits are saved automatically. UmbraOS remembers everything so you never have to explain twice.

Local database · Smart search · Instant recall

09

Watch and learn from you

Highlight text for instant explanations. Or say "take over" and UmbraOS will do the task exactly how you would.

Screen capture · Live analysis · Learn your style

10

Connect to 1,000+ apps

Works with Discord, Notion, GitHub, email, and hundreds more. Send messages, update projects, manage files — all automated.

Universal connectors · Instant setup · Works everywhere

11

Join your meetings

Joins Zoom, Meet, or Teams calls. Listens, finds answers from your files, and can even speak for you with a synthetic voice.

Video calls · Live listening · Synthetic voice

12

Virtual phone number

Get a dedicated number for AI-powered calls, texts, and notifications. Available on the Ultimate plan.

Phone number · SMS · AI calls · 24/7 availability
