Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser when you visit a website. They are used to remember your preferences and improve your experience.

2. Does UmbraOS use cookies?

Our website does not use cookies. We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party tracking cookies.

The only thing stored on your device is the cookie consent preference (if you choose to interact with our cookie banner), which is a single local storage flag — not a tracking cookie.

3. What about UmbraOS software?

UmbraOS software: UmbraOS is desktop and cloud software. It does not use browser cookies. Local data is stored on your device in encrypted databases. Cloud data is processed on our secure servers.

4. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites (e.g., GitHub). We are not responsible for their cookie practices. Please review their privacy policies directly.

5. Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Since we do not set cookies, there is nothing to opt out of on our website.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
